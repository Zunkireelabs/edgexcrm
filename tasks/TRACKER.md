## How we track work (HARD RULE for every developer)
Full SOP: `~/Projects/zunkireelabs-org/SOPs/developer-workflow/README.md` §4. Short form for this repo (key `EDGEX`):
1. Your work is in Plane before you start it, with an assignee, however small.
2. Search before you create. No duplicates.
3. `tasks/TRACKER.md` is generated by `/project-pm sync`. Never hand-edit it; never copy it into Plane.
4. No other to-do lists: not in `todo.md`, `STATUS-BOARD.md`, `FEATURE-ROADMAP.md`, `*-BRIEF.md`, handoffs, PR descriptions or logs. A to-do found anywhere else becomes a ticket.
5. Move the ticket when the work moves (Backlog → Todo → In Progress → In Review → On Stage → Done).
6. Branch `feature/EDGEX-123-short-name`, PR title `EDGEX-123: what it does`, PR targets `stage`. No id, no merge.
7. New work found mid-task is a new ticket, not scope creep.
8. Questions only the owner can answer are `DECIDE …` tickets, assigned to the owner.
9. Unverified isn't done: anything you can't prove gets `needs-verification`.
10. Never delete a ticket. Won't do → Cancelled, with a comment saying why.
11. Handoffs come through tickets, not copy-pasted prompts or handoff files.
12. Push, merge, and any write to a shared or production database need a human's explicit OK (merge approvers: Sadin and Anish), whatever a ticket says.
