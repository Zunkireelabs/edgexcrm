Dialogs v1 (EDGEX-145), for Sadin's approval. One standard for ALL dialogs, copying Attio: the same two-layer shell as the approved command dialog (a frosted 4px outer layer with 16px corners around a white inner card with 12px corners and a hairline), no grey page overlay, a 48px header (icon, title, grey context, ✕), and a 52px footer (left: Back, toggles or hints; right: Cancel, then the main button, which names the action and shows its key ↵ or ⌘↵). Three widths: small 440 (confirms), medium 560, large 720 (lists and forms).
  • Enroll in sequence becomes a list you search (like Move stage and Assign) → preview → start, instead of a dropdown. Everything it does today stays: the skipped counts, what to do with leads already in a sequence (Skip / Switch / Queue), the send-window, daily-cap and real-leads warnings, typing ENROLL for big batches, live progress with Stop, and downloading the skipped leads. The disabled grey “Start” becomes the named blue button.
  • Confirms: a small card with no header bar; destructive ones get a red icon and a red button. Archive keeps its reason picker.
  • Form fields (updated 2026-11-11, measured from Attio’s Create Person): labels 12px/500 at 50% black, 32px fields with 9px corners and a 6% hairline, 14px/500 text, placeholder at 50% black, focus = 1px blue line only (no glow). Applies to fields in every dialog.
  • Dropdowns (Attio, app-wide): white popover, 12px corners, soft shadow; a search row on every dropdown (consistent, like Attio); 32px rows with 8px corners; grey hover; the chosen item gets a grey check (no blue fill); optional grey second line; a “+” action row at the bottom when one exists. Triggers stop being pills.
  • Form dialog size (Attio Create Person, measured): 808px wide (800px card), top fixed at 14% of the window, card a FIXED 70% of the window tall (it does not shrink for short forms), so long forms scroll inside with the header and footer staying put; 44px header and footer, 28px buttons. No description line under the title.
  • Forms (Add lead shown; it keeps the fields it has today, only the look changes): Attio-style field labels, hairline inputs, a blue focus ring, red error text under the field, and “Add another”.
  • How it's built: restyle the two shared parts (ui/dialog, 113 files, and ui/alert-dialog, 11 files) so every dialog in the app upgrades at once. Enroll, Archive and Add lead also get the layout shown here. Other dialogs keep their own content and only gain the new shell, header and footer.
  • Not in this round: browser confirm() pop-ups (none left in .tsx), sheets and side panels, and dark mode (no switch yet).
